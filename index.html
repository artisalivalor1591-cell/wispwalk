⚠️ Play at your own risk
  • Participation is voluntary and entirely at your own risk.
  • Obey all laws, posted signs, and park rules.
  • Stay on public land and open trails. Never enter private property, closed areas, or land posted ‘No Trespassing’ without the owner’s permission.
  • Respect park hours, closures, and seasonal restrictions.
  • Never enter railroad tracks, roads, or water.
  • If the compass points onto private land, go around on public paths, or simply end the hunt. No find is worth crossing a fence.
  • Pins are auto-generated and may be inaccessible. Reroll rather than trespass.
  • The creator is not liable for any injury or property damage.
  • Minors must play with parental supervision.
